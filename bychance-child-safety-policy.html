Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy - ByChance: Dating

Our definitive stance, preventive architecture, and zero-tolerance enforcement against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

Application: ByChance: Dating • Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026
Zero-Tolerance Statement ByChance: Dating maintains an uncompromising zero-tolerance policy towards Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). Any attempt to share, transmit, store, promote, or solicit abusive content or engage in child exploitation will result in immediate termination of the account, permanent platform bans, and expedited reporting to law enforcement authorities including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

01. Overview & Platform Purpose

ByChance: Dating is an online dating and social discovery application designed solely and exclusively for consenting adult individuals aged 18 years or older. The safety and well-being of all individuals—and in particular minors—is of paramount importance to ByChance: Dating.

This Child Safety Standards Policy outlines our organizational measures, operational guidelines, and legal commitments to prevent, identify, eradicate, and report any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation on or through ByChance: Dating.

02. Strict Prohibition of CSAM and CSAE

ByChance: Dating expressly prohibits any representation, creation, dissemination, or discussion of:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual depiction, photography, illustration, video, animation, or digital rendition involving a minor engaged in sexually explicit or suggestive conduct.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Any act of grooming, extortion, coercion, trafficking, or solicitation involving or targeted at an underage individual.
  • Underage Participation: Individuals under the age of 18 are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, browsing profiles, exchanging messages, or participating in any community features within ByChance: Dating.
  • Attempted Facilitation: Using communication features, bio text, voice/video calls, or media sharing within ByChance: Dating to facilitate contact with, exploitation of, or predation on minors is unequivocally forbidden.

03. Age Restrictions & Registration Safeguards

To uphold the adult-only nature of our platform, ByChance: Dating employs stringent access controls:

  • Mandatory Age Verification: All prospective users must confirm that they are at least 18 years of age during registration.
  • Immediate Profile Purge: If any account is suspected or identified as belonging to a minor, ByChance: Dating immediately suspends the profile, revokes access tokens, and permanently deletes all associated personal data from public platform access in compliance with applicable child privacy laws (e.g., COPPA and GDPR).
  • Suspicious Account Screening: Automated indicators and risk heuristics flag profiles whose imagery or bio text suggests underage presence for priority manual review.

04. In-App Moderation & Safety Measures

ByChance: Dating uses multi-layered automated and human mechanisms to monitor, prevent, and eradicate harmful content:

  • Automated Media Scanning & Hash Matching: Uploaded media (profile pictures, photos, and shared assets) is screened against perceptual hashing systems and recognized abusive image databases to detect and intercept known illicit imagery before it can circulate.
  • Text and Keyword Filters: Chat transcripts, status updates, and biographical descriptions are monitored in real time using algorithmic filters configured to detect suspicious keywords, predatory overtures, and grooming patterns.
  • Human Review Moderation: A dedicated trust and safety team reviews escalated reports around the clock (24/7). Content flagged for child safety violations is quarantined instantaneously.
  • End-to-End Audit Logs: Cryptographic audit trails and connection logs are securely maintained to support official legal inquiries and criminal investigations.

05. Law Enforcement & NCMEC Reporting

In adherence to applicable international regulations and statutory requirements (including 18 U.S.C. § 2258A), ByChance: Dating commits to prompt and proactive reporting:

  • Mandatory NCMEC CyberTipline Reports: Whenever ByChance: Dating obtains actual knowledge or reasonable suspicion of child sexual abuse material or child sexual exploitation, a comprehensive report is submitted without delay to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).
  • Domestic & International Law Enforcement Cooperation: We actively collaborate with local, national, and international law enforcement agencies (such as INTERPOL, Europol, and regional investigative divisions), delivering vital transaction logs, IP addresses, account identifiers, and evidence upon lawful legal process.
  • Evidence Preservation: When a child safety incident occurs, all relevant forensic data is locked and retained in an isolated, tamper-evident repository for authorized legal requests.

06. In-App User Reporting Mechanisms

Users of ByChance: Dating are empowered to flag violations instantly directly inside the mobile application:

1
Navigate to Profile or Chat

Open the user's profile card or the active conversation screen within ByChance: Dating.

2
Tap "Report User"

Click on the options menu (⋮ icon) located in the upper right-hand corner and select "Report".

3
Select Category & Submit

Select "Child Safety / Underage Account" or "Inappropriate Content". Provide optional details and submit. Reports marked with child safety categories are routed to high-priority moderation queues.

08. Disciplinary Action & Penalties

Any individual found in breach of this Child Safety Standards & CSAE Policy on ByChance: Dating will face severe and uncompromising consequences:

  • Immediate and irreversible revocation of user account and active subscriptions without refund.
  • Hardware, phone number, and IP-level blacklisting preventing future re-registration on ByChance: Dating.
  • Forensic compilation and referral of full user records to relevant judicial, law enforcement, and child protection agencies.
